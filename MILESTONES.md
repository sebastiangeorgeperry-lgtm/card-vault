# CARD VAULT — Milestones

*A Real Store of Value · Mt. Pleasant, SC*

The rule: finish a phase before starting the next one. Check the boxes as you go.

---

## Phase 1 — The Pitch (this week)

- [ ] Present the pitch deck to Mom & Dad
- [ ] Show them the website (`CARD_VAULT_store_13.html`)
- [ ] Get YES #1: Dad owns the accounts (Shopify, eBay, email)
- [ ] Get YES #2: a parent comes to every Vault Day
- [ ] Put the teaser in Friday's newspaper: "The Card Vault is coming. Follow @handle."

**Done when:** both YESes + teaser printed.

---

## Phase 2 — The Product (next 2 weeks)

- [ ] Pick the launch vault: your best 20–30 cards ($5+)
- [ ] Photo day: window light, dark shirt background, iPhone, no flash
- [ ] Fronts of everything, backs of the expensive ones
- [ ] Put photos in a `cards` folder in Downloads → Claude loads them into the site
- [ ] Review Claude's suggested prices, fix any that feel wrong
- [ ] Pick the Instagram handle and lock it (parent creates account)
- [ ] Practice 5 appraisals with the Vault Scanner on your own cards

**Done when:** the site shows real photos and real prices.

---

## Phase 3 — Go Online (weeks 3–4)

- [ ] Put the site where people can reach it (Dad + Claude set this up)
- [ ] Announce in the newspaper: full ad with the site + handle
- [ ] First Instagram posts: 3 best cards, one per day
- [ ] Watch the "Reserve my holds" emails come in
- [ ] Track every hold in a list: who, what, when

**Done when:** the first hold email arrives from someone who isn't family.

---

## Phase 4 — First Vault Day (about 2 months in)

- [ ] Pick the date (then set `VAULT_DAY` in the site so the countdown starts)
- [ ] Parent locks the venue: coffee shop, church hall, or library room
- [ ] Newspaper ad with the date + $1-of-credit coupon in every copy
- [ ] Build the bulk binder: commons at $1 (eBay bulk lot with Dad if needed)
- [ ] Print a sign with the appraisal rule: "We pay 50% of market in Vault Credit"
- [ ] Pack the kit: cards, binders, Vault Scanner (iPad), cash box (parent), sign
- [ ] Run the day: holds picked up, trade-ins appraised, new holds opened

**Done when:** the first kid walks away with a card they held online.

---

## Phase 5 — The Loop (every month after)

- [ ] Post the Vault Day recap (best pulls, happy traders)
- [ ] Load new inventory + trade-ins into the site within 3 days
- [ ] Newspaper: next date + card of the month
- [ ] Watch the numbers: holds placed, show-up rate, credit spent vs issued
- [ ] Move to Shopify when holds get too big to track by email

**Done when:** it runs the same every month without panic.
