CONDITION GUIDE

Near Mint — fresh from the pack. Sharp corners, no scratches. Pays full.

Lightly Played — tiny corner wear or light scratches you only see up close. Takes off 15%.

Played — clear edge whitening, scratches, maybe a soft crease. Takes off 30%.

Rough — creases, bends, writing, or water marks. Takes off 50%.

Check corners first, then hold it flat under the light for scratches. When you're not sure, pick the lower grade — you can always be generous on purpose.
